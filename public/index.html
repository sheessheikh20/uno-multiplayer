UNO Rules

⚡ Special House Rules

+4 pe +2 Allowed: Counter attacks with +2 or +4 to stack the penalty onto the next player!

UNO Penalty: If caught not saying UNO with 1 card left, penalty = total players in the match cards!

Winners: 5+ players = Top 2 Winners, 2-3 players = 1-2 Winners. No points system!

🎴 Basic Gameplay

Match cards by Color, Number, or Action Symbol. Wild cards can be played anytime.

If you don't have a card, tap the Draw deck. First players to empty their hands win the podium!
